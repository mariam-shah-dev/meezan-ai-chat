What Meezan Means

Four pillars behind every rupee we handle.

Shariah-compliant banking isn't a feature added on top. It's the frame every product is built inside.

Riba-free by design

No interest charged or paid. Profit comes from real trade and shared business.

Asset-backed

Every financing is tied to a real thing, like a home, a car or a shipment. Never money lent against money.

Shared risk

In Musharakah and Mudarabah, gains and losses move together. The bank stands beside you, not above you.

Ethically screened

A Shariah board reviews every product and keeps out sectors that aren't permissible.

Products

Everyday banking, built the Shariah-compliant way.

Each product swaps a conventional structure for an Islamic contract that does the same job, fairly.

Ijarah

Car Financing

The bank buys the car and leases it to you. Ownership moves to you at the end. Rent, not interest.

Diminishing Musharakah

Home Financing

You and the bank co-own the house. Each payment buys a little more of the bank's share, until it's fully yours.

Musharakah

Business Financing

Capital and risk are shared with the business owner, and profit is split by a ratio agreed in advance.

Qard & Mudarabah

Current & Savings

A current account works like a safe loan to the bank. A savings account is a partnership where you get a real share of profit.

Questions

Common questions about Islamic banking.

Every product is built around a real asset or a shared business, not an interest-bearing loan. Profit comes from trade, rent or partnership, not from lending money to get more money back.

Riba means interest: a fixed extra amount on money lent, no matter what happens. Islamic finance links return to real economic activity and shared risk instead.

A Shariah board checks every investment and keeps out sectors like conventional finance, alcohol, gambling and other non-permissible businesses.

Under Mudarabah, you provide the money and the bank invests it in Shariah-compliant work. Real profit is shared at a ratio agreed in advance, and the rate is announced each month instead of being fixed.

Zakat is 2.5% of your net wealth (what you own minus what you owe right now), if it stays above the nisab for one lunar year. Use the Zakat calculator above for an estimate.
